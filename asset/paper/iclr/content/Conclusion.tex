\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

Kimodo-Policy adapts pretrained text-to-motion priors for visually grounded, closed-loop humanoid control through root--body--hand generation and continual visual and state feedback. Experiments show strong results on HumanoidArena, SIMPLE, and eight real-world tasks with 92M trainable parameters out of 678M total. Stronger priors, more VLA data, and greater capacity improve performance within the evaluated settings. Transfer beyond SONIC remains unverified; Appendix~\ref{sec:limitations_future} details this limitation and future directions in controller compatibility, multimodal integration, motion pretraining, and direct transfer from human T2M backbones.
