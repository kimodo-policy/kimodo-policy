\subsection*{AI use statement}

Large language models (LLMs) supported the preparation of this manuscript through language editing and writing assistance. Their use included rephrasing sentences, correcting grammar, improving readability, and refining the organization and presentation of the text. LLMs also assisted in drafting and revising selected passages using technical descriptions, experimental findings, and arguments supplied and verified by the authors.

All research decisions and methodological choices were made by the authors, who conducted the experiments and data analyses and verified the resulting interpretations and conclusions. The authors reviewed all AI-assisted material and take full responsibility for the scientific content and final manuscript, including any text or ideas developed with AI assistance.
